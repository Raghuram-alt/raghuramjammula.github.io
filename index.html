transcribe makes video a little easier to read, revisit, and share. A quiet workspace for people who like to learn.
We turn available YouTube captions into readable, timestamped text and a short extractive summary. Captions may contain mistakes; check the original video when accuracy matters.
The reading room includes original editorial samples so you can explore the experience.
